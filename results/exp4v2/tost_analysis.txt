Method Notes
================================================================================
TOST procedure for two independent proportions (Schuirmann 1987).
Two one-sided z-tests:
  H0_lower: p_A - p_B <= -δ   (lower equivalence boundary)
  H0_upper: p_A - p_B >=  δ   (upper equivalence boundary)
Equivalence declared when BOTH nulls rejected at α=0.05,
equivalently when the 90% CI for (p_A - p_B) lies entirely within [-δ, +δ].
SE computed as sqrt(p1(1-p1)/n1 + p2(1-p2)/n2) using observed proportions.
p_TOST = max(p_lower, p_upper).
